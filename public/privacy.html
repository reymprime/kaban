Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

The short version

Kaban does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data. Everything you save in Kaban stays on your own device. There are no accounts, no servers, no third-party analytics, and no ads. Kaban never sends your activity anywhere.

Where your data lives

All prompts, links, notes, folders, and settings you create are stored locally on your device using your browser's IndexedDB storage. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export or share it.

Backups and shared files

When you use Export backup or Select to Share, Kaban generates a JSON file on your device. You have full control over where that file goes. Kaban never uploads it anywhere on its own.

What we don't do

  • No personal information collected
  • No third-party analytics or remote tracking
  • No cookies for tracking
  • No third-party advertising
  • No account or sign-up required
  • Nothing you do in Kaban is ever sent off your device

External links

Stored Links you save (e.g. TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram) open in your browser or the corresponding app. Once you leave Kaban, the privacy policy of that platform applies.

Privacy Shield

Kaban includes an optional Privacy Shield that helps block common third-party tracking and ad-network requests, protecting your privacy and reducing mobile data use. It works only within Kaban and never affects other apps or your device as a whole.

The Shield keeps two simple counters — how many trackers it has blocked and an estimate of data saved — stored only on your device so you can see it working. These numbers are never uploaded, and you can reset them or turn the Shield off anytime in Settings.

Link safety check

When you open a saved link, Kaban checks the web address on your device against simple patterns common to scam or ad-heavy pages, and may show a warning first. This check happens entirely on your device — no link you save is ever sent anywhere for analysis.

Data deletion

Since all data is stored on your device, you can delete it at any time — remove individual cards inside the app, or clear the app's site data / uninstall the app to erase everything. Kaban has no copy of your data to delete on any server, because none exists.

Children's privacy

Kaban does not collect data from anyone, including children. The app is a general-audience productivity tool.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
